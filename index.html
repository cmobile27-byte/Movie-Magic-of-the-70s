Introduction

Before CGI transformed the film industry, movie magic was built by hand. In the 1970s, filmmakers began pushing the limits of imagination using miniatures, animatronics, and groundbreaking camera techniques.

This decade marked a turning point where practical effects became more ambitious, immersive, and believable than ever before. From mechanical sharks to galaxies far, far away, the innovations of the 1970s laid the foundation for the visual effects we see today.
