Legacy of the 70s

The 1970s didn't just change how movies looked, they changed how they were made. By combining imagination with mechanical innovation, filmmakers created effects that felt real long before computers could generate them. Today, even in an age dominated by CGI, directors continue to draw from these techniques to ground their stories in realism, proving that the legacy of the 1970s is not just visible, but essential.
